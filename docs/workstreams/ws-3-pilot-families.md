# WS-3 — Pilot Families

**Status:** OPEN · **Owner:** Khaled (PO) · **Needed by:** Week 10 · **Opened:** 2026-09-30

## Target

**~25 families** (range 20–30). No confirmed acquisition channel or academy partnership
exists yet.

## Composition needed

The pilot must exercise the product, not just fill seats:

- Children across the 6–15 range
- Mixed memorization levels — beginners through advanced
- A small number of **pre-reading learners** (Mode B / talqeen)
- Mixed curricula: some following a school/maktab plan, some parent-defined
- Arabic-speaking households (primary market)

## Candidate channels

- Personal and family network
- Local masjid and Qur'an halaqat
- Qur'an academies — also a possible WS-1 and WS-2 channel
- Parent communities and schools
- Community groups

## What families are asked to commit

- Consent to child audio processing under the stated privacy terms
- Roughly 10–20 minutes of daily practice
- Feedback on accuracy, usefulness, and trust
- Willingness to be contacted about a paid subscription afterwards

## Success criteria measured during the pilot

| Category | Measure |
|---|---|
| Activation | Parent completes onboarding and assessment; learner starts first session |
| Engagement | ~4+ sessions per week for active learners |
| Retention | D7 and D30 active learners |
| Learning | Recitation accuracy, retention, planned revision completion, repeated-error reduction |
| Parent value | Understands status; finds recommendations useful; feels effort saved |
| Trust | **No Qur'an text incidents. No fabricated religious feedback. Appropriate abstention.** |
| Human review | Escalation rate; reviewer/AI agreement; recurring error categories |
| Commercial | Willingness to pay; conversion intent |

These are **pilot validation criteria**, not guaranteed business outcomes.

## Dependency

Onboarding pilot families requires production infrastructure, which is **blocked on WS-4**.
Recruitment can proceed in parallel; onboarding cannot.
